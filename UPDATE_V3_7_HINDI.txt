MoneyWise v3.7 Monitoring & Reminder Update

1. CBO CFL ACCESS
- CBO login ki Consultant ID / Official Email / Name Cbo Master se match hoti hai.
- Cbo Master ka District uthta hai.
- CFL Master me same District ke saare CFL dropdown me aate hain.
- "Additional Cfl also" access ke liye use nahi hota.

2. FIELD LAST VISIT
- AM/CBO form ke top par "Your Last Visit" card.
- Last CFL, District, Date, Report Type, open/overdue count.
- Last visit ke Observation, Suggestion/Action, Timeline aur TAT point-wise.
- "Visit this CFL again" se same CFL select kiya ja sakta hai.

3. SAME CFL REVISIT FOLLOW-UP
- CFL select karte hi us CFL ke ALL unresolved older action points carry forward honge.
- Sirf immediately previous visit tak limited nahi.
- Completion Date next verification visit par hi bharna hai.

4. ADMIN MONITORING
Admin Panel > Monitoring & Reminders:
- Officer-wise visits
- CFL/Session visit count
- Last visit place/date
- Total action points
- Covered/completed points
- Open, Amber, Red/TAT
- Completion %
- CFL-wise last visit and follow-up status

5. EMAIL REMINDERS
- Admin officer row se manual consolidated Email Reminder bhej sakta hai.
- Full ADMIN daily 8 AM automatic reminders Enable/Disable kar sakta hai.
- Ek officer ko ek consolidated email me sab overdue points milte hain.
- Active Admin IDs ko daily summary email milti hai.
- Reminder Log sheet me audit save hota hai.

6. PDF
- Blue PDF section headings center aligned.
